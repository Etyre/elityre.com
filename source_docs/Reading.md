# How I read

The point of reading is to have new thoughts

When I'm reading, I'm aiming to do some combination of 

* Spur new thoughts 
* Learn and internalize new models
* Learn and memorize new facts



Textbooks 

Papers and experimental reports.

Essays and blog posts











Accordingly, I always, take notes as I read. 

I almost always read on my laptop. I prefer the form factor of physical books or e-ink readers, but I can type much faster than I can write
