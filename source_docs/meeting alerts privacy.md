# Meeting Alerts: privacy

Meeting Alerts is a small app I wrote for my own use. It runs only on my own Mac and I am its only user.

- It reads my own Google Calendar, with read-only access, so it can show me a reminder ten minutes before each meeting.
- The titles and times of the meetings it has reminded me about are kept in a file on my Mac for a day, and in a log on my Mac.
- If I press its timer button, the title of that meeting is sent to my own Toggl account to start a time entry.
- Nothing else is collected, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
