# Social and Political Prirorities

1. Impose a complete domestic ban on frontier AI development.
2. Negotiate a treaty with China to halt frontier AI development worldwide, overseen by an international body and observers from both countries, backed by verification mechanisms.
3. End factory farming: Raise the humane standards of animal agriculture to the point of making factory farming, as practiced today, substantially illegal.
4. Electoral reform: ban gerrymandering, push for approval voting in several US states, lay groundwork for a constitutional amendment 
5. Create capacity for governance inovation: Set up several special economic zones for founding charter small charter cities which can experiment with various kinds of governance innovations: quadratic voting, liquid democracy, policy prediction markets, futarchy, etc.
6. Dismantle large parts of the federal bureaucracy and pass power back down to local city and state governments.
7. Radical housing deregulation: I want the market to build enough units that the price of rent in most American cities drops by half. SF should look like Shenzhen. 
8. Cryonics for all: Establish a free public option for aldehyde-stabilized cryopreservation, along with public infrastructure for maintaining and protecting those awaiting revival. If the government should subsidize anything, it should subsidize protection from the specter of eternal death. 
9. Attempt to come to an accord with China in which the US and China peacefully maintain the world order together, instead of competing politically.
