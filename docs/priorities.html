Social and Political Prirorities

These are earth's priorities, as I see them, in aproximate order of urgency and importance.

  • Stop AI development; Accelerate AI diffusion
  • End factory farming
  • Surmount the meta-institutional bottleneck
  • Cryonics for all
  • Radical housing deregulation
  • Equality before the law
  • End aging
  • Ascend

Stop AI development; Accelerate AI diffusion

Negotiate a treaty with China and the other great powers to halt frontier AI development, including training runs and but especially algorithmic progress, worldwide, overseen by an international body and observers from both countries, backed by verification mechanisms. Exapand the deployment of AI models developed before 2027.

End factory farming

Raise the humane standards of animal agriculture to the point of making factory farming, as practiced today, substantially illegal, or accelerate the development of human alternatives until they dominate factory farmed animal products on cost and consumer preference.

Surmount the meta-institutional bottleneck

Create ways to test and implement the best governance and institution-design ideas we have, and let the best ones win and spread.

Almost all the problems in our world are directly downstream of inadequate institutions, which incentivize those problems, or at least fail to incentivize their solutions. For virtually any part of the world that sucks, you can point to poor institution design that is giving rise to sub-adequate outcomes.

And there’s a general problem, that institutions are self-entrenching. Often in order to reform a system, you have to accrue power within that system. But

  • Set up special economic zones for founding charter small charter cities which can experiment with various kinds of governance innovations: quadratic voting, liquid democracy, policy prediction markets, futarchy, etc.
  • Electoral reform: ban gerrymandering, push for approval voting in several US states, lay groundwork for a constitutional amendment
  • Dismantle large parts of the federal bureaucracy and pass power back down to local city and state governments.

Cryonics for all

Establish a free public option for aldehyde-stabilized cryopreservation, along with public infrastructure for maintaining and protecting those awaiting revival. If the government should subsidize anything, it should subsidize protection from the specter of eternal death.

Radical housing deregulation

I want the market to build enough units that the price of rent in most American cities drops by half. SF should look like Shenzhen.
